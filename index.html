Our Story

Three generations, one apiary, zero shortcuts.

Amber & Comb began in 1994 with two borrowed hives and a stubborn belief that honey should taste like the place it came from. Today, our family still walks the same meadows every dawn, checking on colonies we know by name.

We extract cold, strain lightly, and bottle within 48 hours of harvest — so what reaches your table is as close to the comb as honey can get. No blending, no pasteurizing, no shortcuts that trade flavor for shelf life.

— The Hollis Family, Beekeepers

From Hive to Table

Four steps. No detours.

01

Forage

Bees gather nectar across 40 acres of unsprayed wildflower meadow.

02

Harvest

Frames are pulled by hand only once fully capped and ripe.

03

Cold-Extract

Spun and strained gently — never heated above hive temperature.

04

Bottle

Jarred within 48 hours and labeled with its exact harvest date.

Why Raw Matters

Unheated means unchanged.

Pasteurized honey trades nutrients and flavor for shelf appeal. We skip that trade entirely.

Enzymes Intact

Cold extraction preserves the natural enzymes heat processing destroys.

True Pollen Trace

Unfiltered means the local pollen naturally present stays in the jar.

Traceable Origin

Every jar is dated and tied to the exact meadow it was harvested from.

No Additives

Just honey. No corn syrup, no fillers, no blending with imported stock.
